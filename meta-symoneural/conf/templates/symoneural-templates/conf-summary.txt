Welcome to the SymoNeuRaL Build Environment!
============================================

Your environment is set up for the SymoNeuRaL Distro.

Supported Machines:
  * alienware-r11       (Default: Desktop / NVIDIA RTX 5070 Ti)
  * rpi4-64             (Raspberry Pi 4 64-bit)
  * generic-x86         (Generic UEFI x86_64)

Common Build Targets:
  bitbake symoneural-image-base   (Standard console image)
  bitbake symoneural-image-gui    (Full desktop image with UI)
  bitbake symoneural-image-dev    (Development image with tools)

Useful Commands:
  bitbake -c menuconfig virtual/kernel   (Configure Linux Kernel)
  bitbake-layers show-layers             (List active layers)
  runqemu qemux86-64                     (Run emulation)

Happy Hacking!
